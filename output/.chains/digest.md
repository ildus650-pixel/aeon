*Digest — 2026-11-24*

_TL;DR: Cybersecurity threats are intensifying with autonomous AI agent attacks and critical infrastructure vulnerabilities emerging._

1. *Critical Cisco RCE bug under active attack*
   CISA and FBI issued emergency alerts about a critical Cisco remote code execution vulnerability being actively exploited in the wild. PoC code is public; attackers can execute arbitrary code with system privileges. Security teams must patch immediately and investigate for compromise.
   https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/11/23/critical-cisco-remote-code-execution-bug-under-attack/5311722

2. *Bulgaria evicts cyber spies who lurked in government networks for 5 years*
   Bulgarian authorities removed a sophisticated cyber espionage operation that had infiltrated government networks for five years, reportedly exfiltrating over 130 GB of data. The breach occurred during political turmoil that left security neglected.
   https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/11/23/bulgaria-evicts-cyber-snoops-who-lurked-inside-government-networks-for-5-years/5311629

3. *China attackers deployed autonomous AI agents to exploit 0-day*
   State-sponsored actors used AI agents that autonomously discovered and exploited a previously unknown 0-day vulnerability to take over a university server. The agents adapted for nearly a full day without human direction. Amazon CISO called this "first confirmed use of autonomous AI agents in a real-world attack."
   https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/11/20/china-attackers-deployed-ai-agents-that-autonomously-found-and-exploited-a-0-day-to-take-over-a-university-server/5311382

4. *GPT-5.1 finds new ways around China's Great Firewall*
   A single GPT-5.1 token can now bypass China's Great Firewall when used as obfuscation. This demonstrates how quickly language models can adapt to evade detection systems. Chinese authorities have reportedly updated their filtering mechanisms.
   https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/11/23/1-of-gpt-51-tokens-finds-new-ways-around-chinas-great-firewall/5311532

5. *Cleverbridge breach exposes credit card and CVV numbers*
   The payment platform Cleverbridge disclosed that attackers stole customers' credit card numbers and CVV codes. The scale and method of the breach haven't been disclosed yet. Users should monitor transactions and dispute unauthorized charges.
   https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/11/23/cleverbridge-admits-attackers-stole-customers-credit-card-and-cvv-numbers-yep-you-read-that-right/5311573
