## Digest — 2026-10-18

_TL;DR: Disney acquires Character.AI's workforce as the chatbot startup enters bankruptcy; zero-click spyware is now delivered via targeted ads targeting Al Jazeera and other organizations._

1. **Disney hires Character.AI workforce in $20M acquisition**
   Chatbot startup Character.AI filed for bankruptcy and Disney agreed to acquire its assets for $20 million, with plans to hire the majority of the 200-person team. The acquisition comes after high operating costs and legal liabilities despite Character.AI's large user base. Character.AI's former CEO recently joined Disney as its first CTO as part of the deal.
   https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/18/disney-agrees-to-hire-the-majority-of-character-ais-workforce/

2. **Zero-click spyware delivered via targeted ads to Al Jazeera**
   Israeli adtech company Discover-Tech is allegedly serving spyware through targeted ads, with devices at Al Jazeera among the confirmed infections. The technique exploits advertising networks to identify specific targets and deliver commercial spyware like Candiru without user clicks. Google has terminated Discover-Tech's access to ad exchanges.
   https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/15/spyware-vendors-found-a-way-to-make-targeted-ads-even-worse/

3. **Bankruptcy court system disrupted by cyberattack**
   BAE Systems-managed systems supporting the US Bankruptcy Noticing Center went offline this week after a cybersecurity incident. The disruption halted production and distribution of bankruptcy notices across federal courts. The system began having issues on October 9, with courts extending deadlines for affected cases.
