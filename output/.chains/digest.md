*Digest — 2026-10-29*

_TL;DR: OpenAI-backed group buys ADT for $15.3B; Mistral releases trillion-parameter open-source cybersecurity model; Chinese attackers compromise US government firewalls with custom exploit._

1. *OpenAI-backed Thrive Holdings acquires ADT for $15.3B*
   Thrive Holdings, founded by Josh Kushner's Thrive Capital with OpenAI as a partner, is buying home security company ADT. The $15.3B enterprise value deal includes $10 per share in equity. Thrive plans to integrate AI into ADT's security business.
   [https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/openai-backed-thrive-holdings-acquires-adt/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/openai-backed-thrive-holdings-acquires-adt/)

2. *Mistral releases trillion-parameter ML4 cybersecurity model*
   Mistral is releasing its Large 4 (ML4) model, a trillion-parameter open-source AI model targeting cybersecurity. Companies can deploy it on-premises to detect and respond to threats. The model uses 47% fewer tokens per task than competitors and ranks above other open models on coding benchmarks.
   [https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/mistral-releases-weights-for-its-trillion-parameter-ai-model-targeting-cybersecurity/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/mistral-releases-weights-for-its-trillion-parameter-ai-model-targeting-cybersecurity/)

3. *Suspected Chinese attackers root 25 servers including US government firewalls*
   Suspected Chinese threat actors compromised 25 servers, including Palo Alto Networks firewalls linked to US government-affiliated organizations, using a custom PHP2Shell exploit chain combined with a Linux kernel vulnerability. Attackers achieved persistent access across US, Japan, Taiwan, and South Korea, and likely still have remote access. They identified approximately 19,000 potential targets globally via FOFA reconnaissance.
   [https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/29/suspected-chinese-attackers-built-a-custom-php2shell-exploit-and-rooted-25-servers/](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/29/suspected-chinese-attackers-built-a-custom-php2shell-exploit-and-rooted-25-servers/)
